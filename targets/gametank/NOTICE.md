# The audio firmware

`audio_fw.bin.rle` is the audio firmware of the GameTank, built from the
GameTank SDK by Clyde Shaffer (<https://github.com/clydeshaffer/gametank_sdk>).
It is not part of Prog8 or of p8p8, and it is not covered by this
repository's license.

It is included here with the author's permission (given 2026-11-13).  The
SDK had no license at that time; its author said he would probably put it
under the MIT license.  If it has one now, that is the license of this file.

Everything else in this directory, the fonts included, is part of this
library and under its license.
